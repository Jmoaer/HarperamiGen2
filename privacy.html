Privacy Policy

Harperami Gen 2 choir app · Last updated 27 November 2026

What this app is

Harperami Gen 2 is a private app for the members of the Harperami Gen 2 choir. It gives members a song library, a group chat, private messages and a member directory. The app is operated by the choir, not by a company, and it is not used for advertising.

Information we store

  • Account details you enter: full name, username, password, birthdate, age, address, voice part and profile picture.
  • Messages you send: group chat messages and private messages, together with your username, display name and the time each message was sent.
  • Songs and videos: files uploaded by the choir admin for rehearsals.
  • Presence: a timestamp showing that you currently have the app open, so other members can see an online dot.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase project (database and file storage) used by the choir. The app itself keeps your sign-in state, read markers and notification preference on your own device, in the app's local storage.

Who can see it

Members can see the member directory, the group chat and their own private conversations. Private messages are visible only to the two members in that conversation and to anyone with access to the choir's database credentials. The choir admin can manage members and songs.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files other than the files you choose to upload.

Your choices

You can change your profile details, picture and password inside the app at any time. To have your account and messages deleted, contact the choir admin.

Security

Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. Accounts are intended for choir members only; please choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

The app is used by choir members of all ages under the supervision of the choir admin. It does not knowingly collect data from children for any purpose other than running the choir.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the choir admin through the app or at the email address listed on the app's store page.

This policy describes the app as it is published. If the app changes, this page will be updated.
